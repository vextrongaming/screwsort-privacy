Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Developer: VEXTRON GAMING

Game: Screw Sort / Болты по коробкам

Android package: vex.gaming.screwsort

This policy covers our Android puzzle game and messages you send to our support address. You do not need an account to play. We do not operate a server for your game progress.

1. Game data and support

Level progress, puzzle state, booster inventory and preferences are stored on your device. These let you resume your game and keep your settings. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you include so that we can respond. Do not send passwords or payment details.

2. Advertising

The game displays banners and interstitial advertisements and offers optional rewarded videos for boosters. Yandex Mobile Ads provides advertising. It may send available advertising or other device identifiers to Yandex for advertising purposes. Its documented data transmissions use HTTPS/TLS. The game disables location tracking and does not give affirmative consent to personalized advertising.

See the Yandex Privacy Policy and Yandex SDK data disclosure. An advertiser's page or app has its own privacy practices.

The build also includes the Unity Ads mediation adapter for possible future use. Unity Ads placements are not currently configured. If Unity mediation is enabled, we will update the applicable store disclosures and this policy. See Unity's Privacy Policy for that provider's practices.

3. Permissions and choices

The game does not ask you for contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location access. It does not require your name, email address or payment information to play. You can decline rewarded videos and change the available game preferences in settings. English or Russian is selected from your device language. Android may let you reset or delete your advertising ID in its advertising or privacy settings. Advertising may still appear after using these controls.

4. Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until game data is cleared or removed. Android backup settings may restore local data after reinstalling. Clearing game data removes saved progress and booster inventory.

We retain support correspondence as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information sent to us, email vextrongaming.dev@gmail.com and identify the game and your request. We may need information to locate the relevant correspondence. For data controlled by an advertising provider, use its privacy resources or contact us for assistance. We cannot guarantee deletion of data controlled by another provider.

5. Audience

This game is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address so we can review and address it.

6. Security and international processing

No storage or communication method is completely secure. Advertising, email and hosting providers may process information in other countries according to their policies. Your privacy rights depend on applicable law; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. This website and updates

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking embeds to it. GitHub may process technical request information to provide hosting; see its Privacy Statement. We update this policy when relevant practices change and revise the date above.

Privacy and support contact: VEXTRON GAMING — vextrongaming.dev@gmail.com.

Прогресс, состояние головоломки, запас бустеров и настройки хранятся на вашем устройстве. Это позволяет продолжать игру и сохранять предпочтения. Если вы обращаетесь в поддержку, мы получаем ваш email и сведения из сообщения, чтобы ответить. Не присылайте пароли и платёжные данные.

В игре есть баннеры, межстраничная реклама и добровольный просмотр видео за бустеры. Рекламу предоставляет Yandex Mobile Ads. SDK может передавать доступные рекламные и другие идентификаторы устройства Яндексу для рекламных целей. Согласно документации SDK, передача этих данных защищена HTTPS/TLS. Игра отключает отслеживание местоположения и не передаёт положительное согласие на персонализацию рекламы.

Подробнее: политика конфиденциальности Яндекса и сведения об обработке данных SDK. Для сайта или приложения рекламодателя действуют собственные правила.

Доступные игровые предпочтения меняются в настройках. Русский или английский язык выбирается по языку устройства. Для доступа, исправления или удаления отправленных нам сведений напишите на vextrongaming.dev@gmail.com, указав игру и суть запроса. Может потребоваться информация для поиска переписки.
